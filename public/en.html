FAQ

What is vxcl.ai?
A free web tool that analyzes any YouTube link: engagement stats, a why-it-worked breakdown, comment analysis, viral moment detection and a ready-made AI video prompt.

Is it free?
Yes. Core analysis is free; you only sign in with a Google account to see results.

How do I find the viral moment of a video?
Paste the link. The tool reads comments and engagement spikes, marks the seconds viewers reacted to most, and generates a vertical video prompt for that moment.

Does it store my data?
No. Links are analyzed on the fly and not stored; only your Google email is kept for sign-in.
